===== HANDS-ON A: COUNT TOKENS (about 0:08) =====

Rule: tokens = words x 1.3

  3 words   -> about 4 tokens
  8 words   -> about 10 tokens
  85 words  -> about 110 tokens
  500 words -> about 650 tokens

Ask Claude in a NEW chat:
How many tokens are in this sentence: "I will check the answer before I use it." Give your best estimate and tell me how exact it is.

(That sentence has 9 words, so about 12 tokens. Claude gives an estimate, not an exact count. Verify.)

===== HANDS-ON C: PRICE AN EMAIL TASK (0:38 - 0:50) =====

Step 1: New chat. Paste this prompt (about 150 tokens in total, email + prompt):

Summarise this email in 3 bullets, then write a polite reply of about 80 words.

Hi team, I ordered a laptop bag on 5 October and it was supposed to arrive by 10 October. It still has not arrived and the tracking page has not changed for four days. I called the helpline twice and both times I was told to wait another two days. I need the bag for a trip next week. Please tell me where my order is, or send me a replacement quickly. If neither is possible, I would like a full refund. Thank you, Ravi

Step 2: Count the words in Claude's answer. Output tokens = words x 1.3.
  (Roughly: 3 bullets + an 80-word reply is about 120 words, about 150 tokens.)

Step 3: Fill in the table. Prices are per 1 million tokens (check the official pricing page first).

  Model        Input price   Output price
  Haiku 5.5    $0.10         $0.50
  Sonnet 5.5   $2            $10
  Opus 5.5     $4            $20

  cost = (input tokens x input price + output tokens x output price) / 1,000,000

  Example check with 150 tokens in and 150 tokens out:
  Haiku  = (150 x 0.10 + 150 x 0.50) / 1,000,000 = $0.00009
  Sonnet = (150 x 2 + 150 x 10) / 1,000,000     = $0.0018
  Opus   = (150 x 4 + 150 x 20) / 1,000,000     = $0.0036
  For 1,000 emails: about $0.09, $1.80 and $3.60.

Step 4: Ask Claude to challenge itself:
Which parts of your reply might be wrong or invented, and how can I check each one?

===== OPTIONAL IF TIME: DOES THE MODEL MATTER? =====
