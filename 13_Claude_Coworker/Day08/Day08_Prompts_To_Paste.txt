Task: Explain the conflict in simple words. Give 2 options to resolve it, say which you recommend, and what I should ask my teammate first.
Constraints: Do not pick a winner without saying why. Under 150 words plus the final file.
Format: Explanation, 2 options, recommendation, question to ask, final file.

===== 3C. RESOLVE =====
In VS Code choose Accept Current, Accept Incoming or Accept Both above the conflict. Then:
git add k8s/deployment.yaml

Stuck? git merge --abort

===== 4. BRANCHING ADVICE (optional, a good take-home) =====
Role: You are a Git workflow advisor.
Context: A team of 6 engineers. One Terraform repo and one Kubernetes repo. Deploys to staging and production, about twice a week. A small CI pipeline.
Task: Recommend trunk-based development or GitFlow, and say why.
Constraints: Maximum 5 bullets. Say what would change your mind. Do not assume tools we do not have.
Format: One recommendation line, 5 bullets, one line: "What would change my mind".
