A good prompt has 4 parts: Role, Context, Task, Format.

--- 1A. Weak prompt (type this first) ---
What is cloud computing?

--- 1B. Good prompt (start a NEW chat, paste this) ---
You are a friendly teacher. I am new to IT. Explain what cloud computing is using an everyday example, like renting a house. Answer in 5 short lines.

--- 2. Improve the answer with follow-ups ---
First prompt:
Write a short, polite email to my manager asking for leave this Friday for a family function. Keep it under 80 words.

Then type, one by one:
Make it more formal.
Now make it 3 lines only.
Add that I will finish my work by Thursday.

--- 3. Ask, read, verify (replace [your city] with your city) ---
Tell me 3 facts about my city, [your city].

Check one fact on Google or Wikipedia. Then ask Claude:
Which of these facts should I double-check, and where?

--- OPTIONAL: if you already use Linux ---
You are a senior Linux engineer helping a junior colleague. Explain this command piece by piece and tell me what output to expect. Keep it under 150 words.

du -sh /var/log/* | sort -rh | head -5

Then verify in a terminal (or search "du man page"):
man du
du --help
(press q to leave the man page)
